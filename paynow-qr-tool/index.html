PayNow QR
Render · Scan · Decode

QA tool. Render a PayNow QR from the create-API output, then scan/decode it the way a banking app reads it. Staging/dev only.

1Render QR

Paste base64EncodedImage for the exact API PNG, or qrCodeData (EMVCo string) to generate the QR locally.

The QR encodes only the public label (proxy, amount, merchant, reference). It does not contain the internal paynow_qr_codes.external_id.

2Scan & Decode (banking-app view)

Upload a QR image, or paste the EMVCo string / base64. Shows what a payer's bank app would read: payee, amount, proxy, reference.
